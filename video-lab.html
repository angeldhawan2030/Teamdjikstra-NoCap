    About us

    Why No Cap exists

    Most scams work because nobody has ten spare seconds to check. No Cap gives you those ten seconds back.

    The name is the promise: no exaggeration, no fear-selling, no hidden agenda. Every verdict on this site shows the exact signals behind it, so you finish a scan knowing why something is suspicious — and you can spot the next one without us.

    Nothing you paste, upload or scan is kept. Analysis runs, the answer comes back, the input is discarded.

    What we protect you from

    Phishing linksAddresses built to look like your bank, courier or employer.
    Cloned websitesShopfronts and login pages copied pixel for pixel.
    AI-generated imagesSynthetic faces, products and "evidence" photos.
    Deepfake videoFaces and voices pasted onto someone else's body.
    Fake newsUnsigned articles, funded angles and recycled headlines.
    Paid-for reviewsFive-star floods written by accounts that never bought anything.
    Scam messagesWhatsApp and SMS bait: parcels, refunds, job offers, "hi mum".
    Misleading productsListings promising results the product cannot deliver.

    Detection suite

    Seven focused instruments. Each one shows its working: the signals it checked, which ones tripped, and how much each one moved the score.

    Fake Website Detector

    Twelve checks on any URL: plain HTTP, shorteners, bait keywords, raw IP hosts, hyphen stuffing, length, odd characters and lookalike spelling.

    AI Image Detector

    Upload, drag, paste from the clipboard or shoot with the camera. Nova inspects hands, faces, skin, text, crowds, tiles, shadows, reflections and floating objects.

    AI Video Detector

    Frames are pulled from your clip and studied for bent fingers, warping backgrounds, shimmering skin, blink rate, stiff motion, wrong shadows and lip-sync drift.

    Test a clip     04

    Fake News Analyzer

    Who wrote it, what they usually cover, who funds the outlet — and whether the writing leans on shouting, outrage or an old date wearing a new headline.

    Check a story     05

    Fake Review Detector

    Sudden five-star floods, copy-paste enthusiasm, usernames like kev88213, blank avatars, one-brand reviewers and a thin verified-purchase rate.

    A semantic read of any pasted message: manufactured urgency, impossible pricing, money requests, payment rails and the wording scam scripts reuse.
