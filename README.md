# NoCap – AI-Powered Cyber Scam Detection & Awareness Platform

##  Overview

**NoCap** is an AI-powered cybersecurity awareness platform designed to help users identify and avoid online scams, misinformation, and AI-generated content. The platform combines artificial intelligence with rule-based analysis to detect suspicious content while providing clear explanations that help users understand *why* something may be unsafe.

Rather than simply labeling content as "fake" or "real," NoCap educates users about common cyber threats and promotes digital literacy through interactive analysis tools and learning resources.

---

##  Features

###  AI Video Detector

* Upload videos for AI-generated content detection.
* Extracts and analyzes video frames.
* Displays an AI risk score with detailed findings.

---

###  AI Image Detector

* Detects AI-generated or manipulated images.
* Highlights suspicious visual characteristics.
* Provides confidence scores and explanations.

---

###  News Analyzer

* Evaluates news articles for potential misinformation or bias.
* Summarizes the content.
* Identifies credibility indicators and possible manipulation.

---

###  Message Guard

Analyze messages from:

* WhatsApp
* SMS
* Email
* Social Media

Detects:

* Phishing attempts
* OTP scams
* Fake job offers
* Investment scams
* Lottery scams
* Authority impersonation
* Social engineering
* Advance-fee fraud

---

### Fake Website Detector

Scan suspicious URLs for:

* Phishing indicators
* Fake domains
* URL shorteners
* Look-alike websites
* Raw IP addresses
* Suspicious keywords
* Hyphen stuffing
* Insecure HTTP connections

The detector also explains why a website may be considered risky.

---
###  Fake Review Detector

Analyze product and business reviews to identify potentially fake or AI-generated reviews.

Features include:
- Detects overly generic or repetitive review patterns
- Identifies suspicious promotional or misleading language
- Flags possible AI-generated reviews
- Highlights sentiment inconsistencies
- Provides an authenticity score with explanations
- Helps users make informed purchasing decisions

### Cybercrime Learning Hub

An educational section that teaches users about common cyber threats, including:

* Phishing
* Smishing
* Vishing
* QR Code Scams
* OTP Fraud
* Fake Websites
* Deepfakes
* Online Shopping Scams
* Romance Scams
* Investment Scams
* Identity Theft
* Social Engineering

## How It Works

1. Select a detection tool.
2. Upload or paste your content.
3. NoCap performs local heuristic analysis.
4. AI evaluates semantic patterns and suspicious indicators.
5. A risk score and detailed explanation are presented.
6. Users receive practical advice on staying safe.

---

##  Why NoCap?

Unlike many detection tools that only provide a score, NoCap explains **why** content is suspicious. This educational approach helps users recognize scams independently and build long-term cybersecurity awareness.

---

##  Privacy

NoCap is designed with user privacy in mind.
* Uploaded content is analyzed only for the requested detection task.
* Content is not intended to be stored after analysis.
* Local heuristic checks continue to work even if AI services are unavailable.
